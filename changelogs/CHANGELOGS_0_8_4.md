# CR League 0.8.4
Release date: 2026-07-30

## Added
- The Grand Prix recap now appears as a compact, dismissible overlay on the finished circuit map, with its rewards, flag and replay controls together.

## Changed
- Replay rendering does less work per frame for smoother circuit-map animation, especially on mobile.
- The Destiny Wheel creates a newly randomized starting grid every time a race is launched or restarted.

## Fixed
- Fixed visual finish-line slowdowns caused by braking and corner profiles overlapping a main straight.
- Fixed replay result controls so restarting hides the recap until the next finish.
