# CR League 0.8.8
Release date: 2026-07-31

## Added
- The Destiny Wheel's shared link now carries the cars and the colours as well as the names and the circuit pool, so a group opens the same grid, dressed. Links shared before this still work.
- Shuffle every car and livery in the wheel's list at once, or pick a participant's car by clicking it.
- The One to Beat shows your own car beside the rival's, with your colours and your car to choose, both kept from one duel to the next. A circuit pool picker sits with them.
- Space pauses and resumes a replay, and restarts one that has finished.
- A Starter Pack of ten circuits — Boavista, Helsinki, Vltava, Docklands, Ocean Drive, Reforma, the Bund, Seoul, Casablanca, Brisbane — and it is the pool a circuit picker opens on.
- The animated map behind the menus can be swapped for a still, from the profile menu.

## Changed
- The stat pills on cards were too small to read and are now sized to be read, and every one of them explains what it is on hover.
- The changelog opens on the last three releases with a button for the rest, and has a way back out.
- A new rival in a duel turns up in a new car, never the one you are driving.
- The wheel's final order carries the winner's time and each car's gap to the one ahead.

## Fixed
- Fixed an empty map when a duel was opened straight from its address: the circuit drawings had not arrived when the board took its snapshot, and it kept the empty one.
- Fixed the dotted line to the standing you follow disappearing when that standing sat below the fold of a collapsed list — which, on a phone, is where a player finishing fifth found their own car.
- Fixed one car driving backwards on every map.
- Fixed a stray "C" following the car around a circuit preview.
- Fixed the position numbers on the helmet badges being unreadable, and smearing from fourth place down.
- Fixed "reset preferences" leaving the wheel's circuit pool as it was.
- Fixed the circuit being raced dropping out of the catalogue when a filter was on.
