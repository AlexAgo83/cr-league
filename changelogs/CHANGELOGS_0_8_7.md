# CR League 0.8.7
Release date: 2026-07-31

## Added
- The Destiny Wheel can be handed round by link. Set the list up on one screen, hit "Share the list", and everyone else opens the same grid on their own, ready to launch. The link carries the names and the circuit pool.
- Arcade games have their own addresses — `/arcade`, `/arcade/duel`, `/arcade/wheel`. Typing one opens that game cold, and back and forward move between them.
- The One to Beat explains itself when the duel starts: the ring, what engagement buys, and the fact that he is reading you too. With the usual "do not show this again".

## Changed
- Raindrops on the screen now arrive, run down the glass and thin out, instead of landing once and sitting there while the rain falls behind them.
- The wheel's buttons sit in the corner the eye goes to: a way back beside a way on, on two rows. Same for the duel briefing.

## Fixed
- Fixed the share button's icon riding above the words it belonged to, and a row of buttons where one was shorter than the others.
- Removed the circuit count next to the wheel's region picker: the picker already says which pool is on.
